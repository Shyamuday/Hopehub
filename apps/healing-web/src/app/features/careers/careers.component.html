Careers at Hope Hub

Join Hope Hub's care and peer-support network

We are building an accessible mental wellness support network for people who need calm, respectful, and privacy-aware guidance. Share your profile and our team will review fit, availability, and service areas.

Who should apply?

  • Mental wellness professionals and qualified counsellors for paid consultations.
  • Psychology students who want supervised, non-clinical emotional-support-listener experience.
  • Peer supporters with relevant lived experience who respect boundaries and escalation limits.

Selection Steps

  1. 1. Choose the professional or listener path that fits you.
  2. 2. Team reviews your profile, availability, and safeguards.
  3. 3. Professional profiles are verified before paid work is discussed.
  4. 4. Listeners complete orientation before supporting the community.
