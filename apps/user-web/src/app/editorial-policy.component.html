Editorial standards

How we create and review health information.

HopeHub Care publishes educational information to help people prepare for a consultation and understand when professional or urgent offline care may be needed. It does not replace an individual medical assessment.

Last reviewed: 25 August 2026

Who creates our content

Articles and treatment pages may be written by HopeHub clinicians, approved providers, or the editorial team. Where a named author or professional reviewer is available, their name and role are shown on the page. Clinical claims must be reviewed before publication.

Our review process

  • We separate general education from diagnosis, prescription, and emergency advice.
  • We check medical statements against reputable clinical or public-health sources.
  • We avoid guarantees, exaggerated outcomes, and unsupported cure claims.
  • We state important limitations and red-flag symptoms in plain language.
  • We review material when guidance changes or when a concern is reported.

Homeopathy and evidence

Our service is homeopathy-led, but public information must not suggest that homeopathy is a substitute for emergency treatment, necessary diagnostic testing, surgery, vaccination, or other evidence-based care. A doctor may advise physical examination, testing, referral, or conventional treatment when clinically appropriate.

Corrections and feedback

If you find information that is unclear, outdated, or potentially unsafe, contact us through the Contact page. We review reported issues and make material corrections as soon as they are verified. Updates do not change the original publication date unless a page is substantially rewritten.

Medical safety

For chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, stroke signs, severe allergic reaction, or sudden worsening, contact local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency facility. Do not wait for an online reply.
